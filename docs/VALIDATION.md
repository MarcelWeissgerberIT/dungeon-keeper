## Excavation / future-room designation and volumetric creatures

- The rectangle draft supports both excavation-only and future-room orders on known earth/gold or empty floor. Room selection preserves the drawn area; Enter works after mouse selection of a room, and Escape removes the draft. Terrain picking uses the visible surface instead of projecting through a raised wall onto hidden ground.
- Confirmed orders are stored per tile, drawn with coloured grids/room diamonds and displayed on the minimap and order list. A single render pass combines base grid, existing orders and the draft without exceeding one entry per map tile. Removing an order never sells a completed room.
- Six designation tests cover the complete excavation/claim/build sequence, zero-gold waiting and resumption, idempotency and repurposing, cancellation after partial completion, unreachable areas, old-save migration, rejection of invalid/array-valued plans, save/load without double charges, and a full treasury with workers carrying gold. Construction jobs can expand storage even when delivery is blocked; fully loaded workers do not keep mining more gold.
- The original immediate-construction API remains covered by its atomic-budget tests. The new HUD queues work without reserving or charging gold; each finished tile is paid for once by the worker construction path. Pending orders survive saves, while unconfirmed rectangle drafts do not.
- Browser checks include drawing a 2×2 grid containing earth, changing it from excavation to a future archive, confirming with Enter after room-button focus, observing the retained grid and order list without immediate spending, and automatic worker execution. English text and pending-order controls are also covered by the final release review.
- All five creature rigs remain finite, grounded and raycastable. Tests verify independent animation poses, shared meshes and caller-controlled transforms. Per-model rigid parts use 15–22 draw calls, a structural optimization rather than a measured frame-rate guarantee. No additional OpenArt assets or credits were required for this mechanics revision.
- Lint passes for the construction/model modules and new tests. Broader lint still reports existing UI/scaffold conventions (React effect initialization, anchor/image/status markup and a simulation `prefer-const` finding); it is not a release check in the Pages workflow.

## Practical boundaries

One replayable expedition, not a remake of another game's campaign. Resource layouts vary by seed while the starting dungeon stays learnable. Desktop mouse/keyboard is the primary input; narrow displays use a compact command column; secondary passive indicators are reduced on short windows. No multiplayer, cloud saves or imported third-party game assets. Persistent data stays in the local browser; blocked browser storage leaves the live session playable but prevents saves. The build is static and requires no server secrets.
